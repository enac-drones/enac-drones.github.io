Pierre Mazurier is a Research Engineer at ENAC, responsible for developing a web-based visualization platform to support the team's algorithms and research activities. The tool is designed to be versatile to be used on a wide range of applications including general UTM, drone swarms, deconfliction or even drone racing. His work bridges the gap between complex algorithm research and intuitive visual solutions to enhance understanding and usability across various drone related domains.
